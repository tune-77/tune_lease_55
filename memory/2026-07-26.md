## Promotable Items
- Shion positioning boundary: ringi comment analysis can extract past concerns, approval conditions, rejection reasons, and domain-specific warning patterns, but it is only a data source for Shion, not a replacement. Shion's distinct value is judgment lifecycle management: capturing the hesitation and corrections before the formal comment, turning them into quarantined judgment assets, reusing them in future cases, and validating whether they helped after outcome registration. Impact: hackathon and README explanations should avoid making Shion look like a comment-summary AI; frame it as judgment asset DevOps. Next action: use the phrase "稟議書分析は燃料、紫苑は判断のライフサイクル管理" when this objection appears.
- Shion LLM justification: Result labels alone can train prediction models, but they cannot preserve why a human hesitated, what confirmation would change the view, or whether an unselected concern should be revived after the outcome. LLMs make it practical to treat these intermediate judgment words as operational data: decompose them into reasons, risk axes, confirmation conditions, reusable judgment assets, and post-result validation signals. Impact: Shion's strongest technical claim is "LLMだから、人間の判断過程をDevOpsできる." Next action: use this as the answer when judges ask why this was not possible with ordinary scoring or result-only learning.
- Shion judgment culture: A 1000-case review drill is valuable not as mass result-label creation, but as accumulation of judgment events: what Shion saw, what User accepted/changed/rejected, how outcomes differed, and what one-line lesson survived. At scale this records not only rules but a judgment culture: what risks User notices, what conditions make a case acceptable, what kind of loss is not a credit failure, and how User revises after being wrong. Impact: Shion can become a system for preserving and validating a screening judgment culture, not just a rulebook.
- Judgment culture versioning: Screening judgment changes over time with interest rates, industry conditions, insolvency trends, bank stance, used-asset markets, legal/accounting standards, and subsidy/market context. Shion should preserve when a judgment rule changed, why it changed, when old rules still apply, and where old rules become dangerous. Impact: the deeper product definition is "紫苑は、判断文化をバージョン管理するAI." Next action: when explaining long-term value, frame Shion as version control for business judgment culture.
- Shion input strategy: The next product risk is not whether Shion can reason, but whether humans will keep entering the materials needed for judgment culture. Do not assume people will write long logs. The adoption story should be: existing CRM/kintone/Cybozu records provide fact logs via CSV first, API later if allowed; Shion converts those facts into judgment events; humans only add the missing signals that systems rarely capture, such as discomfort, correction, conditional approval reason, and retrospective lesson, preferably on smartphone with low-friction controls. Impact: this answers the judge objection "won't this fail if humans do not input enough?" with a realistic integration path.
